City names are derived from GeoNames cities15000 data, licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0: https://www.geonames.org/export/
The list contains up to 250 of the most populous places per country.
Where GeoNames has no entry, capital names are sourced from
https://github.com/mledoze/countries (ODbL 1.0).
Names are stored as stable ID, Arabic label, and English label. Language-tagged
GeoNames names are preferred. Missing Arabic labels were generated using machine
translation; a small number were transliterated automatically and may be
approximate. The app does not contact a translation service at runtime.
