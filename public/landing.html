Privacy Policy

Introduction

SIDAR AI respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the SIDAR AI website and services.

By accessing or using SIDAR AI, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

SIDAR AI may collect certain types of information in order to provide and improve our services.

Personal Information

When you create an account or contact us, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Account information

Image Data

When you use the AI analysis feature, you may upload photos for analysis. These images are processed by our artificial intelligence systems to generate beauty insights.

SIDAR AI does not use uploaded images for identification or facial recognition purposes.

Usage Information

We may automatically collect certain information when you use the platform, such as:

  • Device type
  • Browser type
  • Usage statistics
  • Interaction with the platform

This information helps us improve the performance and user experience of our services.

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect may be used to:

  • Provide AI-powered skin and hair analysis
  • Generate personalized beauty reports
  • Improve platform performance and user experience
  • Communicate with users regarding service updates
  • Ensure security and prevent misuse of the platform

SIDAR AI does not sell personal information to third parties.

Image Processing and AI Analysis

Images uploaded by users are processed by artificial intelligence systems to analyze skin and hair indicators.

The purpose of this analysis is to provide personalized beauty insights and recommendations.

Images may be temporarily processed to generate reports and improve system performance. SIDAR AI takes reasonable measures to protect this data.

Data Security

SIDAR AI implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no system can guarantee absolute security, and users acknowledge that they share information at their own risk.

Third-Party Services

SIDAR AI may use trusted third-party services to support certain features of the platform, including AI processing and analytics.

These services may have their own privacy policies and data handling practices.

User Rights

Users may request to:

  • Access their personal information
  • Update account details
  • Request deletion of their account

Requests may be submitted through our contact email.

Data Retention

SIDAR AI retains user information only for as long as necessary to provide services, maintain platform functionality, and comply with applicable regulations.

Users may request deletion of their account and associated data.

Children’s Privacy

SIDAR AI services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

SIDAR AI may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect improvements to our services or legal requirements.

Users are encouraged to review this page regularly for updates.

Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, please contact us.
